Modify ONLY the existing Attendance Rules / Annual Leave Balance logic to implement this exact behavior. Do not change Add Employee, authentication, payroll architecture, employee accounts, shifts, or unrelated HR logic.

Required behavior:

1. Annual leave balance settings

- Manager sets the employee's annual leave entitlement, e.g. 21 days.
- Manager selects the specific months in which the annual leave balance is allowed to be used/deducted.
- Example: February, April, June, August, October, December.
- Manager sets a monthly maximum deduction from the annual balance, e.g. 1 or 2 days per month.
- The system must NEVER consume the entire annual balance in one month.
- Maximum deduction per month must always respect the manager-defined monthly limit.

2. Absence WITHOUT permission

- An employee absent without permission must NOT consume the annual leave balance.
- Apply the company's configured unauthorized-absence rule instead.
- Example: if unauthorized absence = 2 days deduction, deduct 2 days according to that rule, NOT from annual leave balance.

3. Absence WITH permission

- If the employee has an approved permission/authorized absence, it can consume annual leave balance.
- It should consume from the annual balance ONLY if the current month is one of the months selected by the manager.
- Example:
  Manager selected February as a balance month.
  Employee has approved permission/absence in February → deduct from annual leave balance according to the configured rule.
- Even if the general permission rule says 1 day, the monthly balance limit still applies.
- Example: monthly limit = 1 → only 1 day can be deducted from annual balance that month.
- If monthly limit = 2 → maximum 2 days can be deducted from annual balance that month.

4. Important distinction
   Keep these as two separate rules:

- Unauthorized absence → company absence penalty/rule, NOT annual balance.
- Approved permission/authorized absence → may deduct annual leave balance, subject to:
  a) selected month
  b) available balance
  c) monthly maximum deduction.

5. Employee visibility
   Employee Self-Service must clearly show:

- Total annual entitlement
- Used from annual balance
- Remaining balance
- Amount deducted this month
- The applicable balance months
- Monthly maximum
- Any unauthorized absence deductions separately from annual leave deductions.

6. Payroll synchronization
   The resulting attendance/absence/leave calculations must continue to flow correctly into payroll without double deduction.

7. Tests
   Add strong regression tests covering at minimum:

- Selected month + approved permission → annual balance deduction.
- Non-selected month + approved permission → no annual balance deduction.
- Unauthorized absence → does NOT consume annual balance.
- Unauthorized absence penalty follows its configured rule.
- Monthly maximum = 1 → never deduct more than 1 annual-leave day in that month.
- Monthly maximum = 2 → never deduct more than 2 in that month.
- Multiple absences in the same month cannot bypass the monthly maximum.
- Annual balance cannot go below zero.
- Different months have independent monthly limits.
- Payroll receives the correct final attendance/leave result.
- Existing historical calculations remain unchanged.
