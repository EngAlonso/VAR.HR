==================================================

Verified issue:

Employee-role requests can still access company-wide data through insufficient server-side role scoping.

Affected areas include:

- Employee data
- Attendance data
- Reports
- Payroll-period data

Leave and permission lists were already scoped correctly.

The problem is therefore inconsistent authorization across domains.

This MUST be fixed server-side.

Do NOT solve this by merely hiding navigation items.

Expected behavior:

Employee:
- Can access only their own employee record.
- Can access only their own attendance.
- Can access only their own leave.
- Can access only their own permissions.
- Can access only their own salary/payroll information.
- Must not access company-wide employee lists.
- Must not access other employees' attendance.
- Must not access company reports.
- Must not access company payroll periods.

Manager:
- Can only access employees within their authorized scope.
- Scope may depend on branches, departments, direct reports, or explicitly assigned employees.
- Backend must enforce this.

Company Owner:
- Full access within their own company according to permissions.

Platform Owner:
- Explicitly privileged platform-level access.

Do not break legitimate access while fixing the employee restriction.

After fixing this, test the affected endpoints.
