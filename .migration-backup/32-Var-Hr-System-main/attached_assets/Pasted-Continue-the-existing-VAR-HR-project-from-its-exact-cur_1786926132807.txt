11. Authorization

Verify server-side permissions.

Employee

Can only access reports/data they are authorized to see.

They must never see:

- Another employee's private payroll.
- Company-wide payroll.
- Unauthorized company reports.

Manager

Can access only the employees within their permitted scope.

They must not automatically receive company-wide access.

Owner

Can access company-wide reports and exports according to the existing authorization model.

Developer

Preserve the existing distinction between technical/system access and payroll/HR business access.

Do not automatically grant payroll visibility to the Developer role.

Cross-company

Every report, export, preview, and import operation must remain company-scoped.

Cross-company access must fail.
