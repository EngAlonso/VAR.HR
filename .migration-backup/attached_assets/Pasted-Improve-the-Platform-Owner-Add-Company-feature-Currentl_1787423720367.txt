Improve the Platform Owner "Add Company" feature.

Currently there is no clear button/action to add a new company. Add a prominent "Add Company" button in the Platform Owner company management area.

When creating a company, the Platform Owner must be able to enter:

Company Information:
- Company name
- Company address
- Currency
- Employee limit

Company Owners:
- Number of company owners
- Dynamically create an owner-account form for each owner based on that number
- For each owner:
  - Full name
  - Username
  - Password
  - Primary phone number
  - Additional/backup phone numbers
  - Email address
  - Additional/backup email addresses

Subscription:
- Monthly subscription price
- Annual subscription price

Important rules:
- The number of Company Owner accounts must exactly match the number entered for owners.
- Each owner must have a separate account and login credentials.
- Passwords are permanent passwords, not temporary or one-time passwords.
- The Platform Owner creates the initial passwords and can later reset them without knowing the old password.
- Do not display or retrieve existing passwords after they are stored.
- Validate usernames, emails, phone numbers, required fields, and duplicate accounts before creating the company.
- The employee limit must be stored and enforced by the existing company rules.
- Store the selected currency with the company.
- Store monthly and annual subscription prices with the company/subscription data.

After successful creation:
- Create the company.
- Create exactly the requested number of Company Owner accounts.
- Create the required subscription record.
- Associate every owner account with the correct company.
- Record the company creation and owner-account creation actions in the existing audit system.
- Show a clear success result with the created company and owner usernames.
- Never expose stored password hashes or existing passwords.

Keep all existing authentication, tenant isolation, Company Owner permissions, Platform Owner permissions, Backup/Restore, and existing staff-account management unchanged.

Do not modify unrelated features.
