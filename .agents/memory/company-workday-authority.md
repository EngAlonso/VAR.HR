---
name: Employee reference compensation rates
description: Which inputs determine compensation rates and which remain attendance-only.
---

For every employee, derive the daily rate from salary ÷ reference workdays and the hourly rate from salary ÷ (reference workdays × reference daily hours). Do not use attendance-rule working days as compensation-rate divisors. Keep attendance rules authoritative for attendance scheduling and absence eligibility. Monthly salary proration remains based on the assigned payroll cycle.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested employee-specific reference days and hours for both monthly and hourly pay, separate from attendance scheduling.

**How to apply:** Use the employee's saved reference fields for profile rates, overtime, lateness, early-departure, and absence rates. Preserve payroll-cycle proration and immutable approved payroll snapshots.
