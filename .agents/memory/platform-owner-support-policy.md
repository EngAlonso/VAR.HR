---
name: Platform-owner support policy
description: Durable goal and safeguards for correcting VAR HR company data through platform support.
---

Platform owners need to resolve common issues for all companies from the VAR HR interface, without code edits. Missing employee reference workdays must use values confirmed by the company; do not assume 26 days or bulk-fill missing values.

**Why:** Reference workdays affect compensation, and company-specific support corrections should not require direct code or database edits.

**How to apply:** Extend the platform-owner support area with allowlisted, company-scoped edits that require a reason and are audited. Recalculate editable payroll snapshots after relevant corrections, but leave drafts for first calculation and approved or locked periods unchanged.
