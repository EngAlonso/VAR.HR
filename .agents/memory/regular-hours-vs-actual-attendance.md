---
name: Regular hours vs actual attendance
description: Distinction between scheduled basic hours and raw elapsed check-in/out time.
---

Attendance displays should distinguish basic working hours from raw elapsed attendance time. Basic hours are the overlap between check-in/check-out and the employee's assigned schedule; time before the schedule or after its end is not basic time.

**Why:** A late arrival should reduce basic hours (for example, 09:30–17:00 is 7.5 basic hours), while early arrival and late departure should not inflate the regular-hours total. Raw duration is still useful for attendance history and overtime qualification.

**How to apply:** Use the schedule-clipped value for attendance and payroll-facing regular-hours displays, and keep the raw worked duration available under a distinct actual-worked-hours meaning.
