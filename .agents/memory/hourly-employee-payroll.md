name: Hourly employee pay
description: Per-employee hourly pay derived from reference salary, workdays, and daily hours.
---

Hourly employees use a per-employee rate derived from the entered monthly reference salary, reference workdays per month, and reference daily hours. Those day/hour values are only the rate divisor, not a work schedule or attendance requirement. Pay the full elapsed interval between corrected biometric check-in and check-out, including breaks, at the base hourly rate. Leave without recorded attendance is unpaid; do not subtract lateness or absence again, and do not apply overtime multipliers. Monthly salary employees keep the existing company rules.

**Why:** The user clarified that hourly employees have no fixed days or schedule, all time between the corrected punches is paid, and reference days/hours only calculate the hourly rate.

**How to apply:** Keep pay basis explicit per employee. Calculate hourly rate as reference salary divided by (reference workdays × reference daily hours), then apply it to actual corrected punch duration. Preserve existing company rules for monthly employees.
