name: Hourly employee pay
description: Per-employee hourly pay derived from reference salary, workdays, and daily hours.
---

Hourly employees use the entered monthly reference salary divided by reference workdays and daily hours. Monthly-paid employees use the same employee-specific reference inputs for hourly and daily compensation rates; their base salary proration still follows the assigned payroll cycle. For hourly-paid employees, the reference values are rate divisors only, not a work schedule or attendance requirement. Pay the full elapsed interval between corrected biometric check-in and check-out, including breaks, at the base hourly rate. Leave without recorded attendance is unpaid; do not subtract lateness or absence again, and do not apply overtime multipliers.

**Why:** The user clarified both that hourly employees have no fixed days or schedule and that employee-specific reference days/hours determine rates across both pay bases.

**How to apply:** Keep pay basis explicit per employee. Calculate hourly rate as salary divided by (reference workdays × reference daily hours), then apply it to actual corrected punch duration for hourly employees. Use salary divided by reference workdays for daily deductions. Preserve monthly-cycle proration and keep schedule rules separate from rate divisors.
